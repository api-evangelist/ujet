## Agentic Experience Orchestration (AXO)

AXO is a transformative architectural framework and UJET's most significant product innovation - the industry's first end-to-end agentic CX solution. AXO introduces a persistent AI layer that sits between customer conversations and enterprise systems, eliminating the long-standing bottleneck of forcing human agents to manually bridge disconnected tools during live interactions.

AXO doesn't replace humans - it supercharges them. Rather than telling agents what to do next, AXO empowers agents to execute complex workflows across disparate systems with a single click, while LLM-based Computer-Using Agents (CUAs) complete the backend work - even when APIs are unavailable.

How AXO works:
- Ingests historical conversations and customer data to deploy autonomous agentic AI virtual agents
- Automates low-value tasks and seamlessly escalates to humans for high-value or emotive interactions
- Upon escalation, AI stays in the loop - surfacing relevant CRM/CDP context, real-time summaries, suggested responses, and next-best action
- Computer-Using Agents execute workflows across back-office systems without requiring API availability
- Syncs structured tickets and summaries back to data lakes, CDPs, and CRMs to maintain a single source of truth
- Continuously learns from interaction outcomes, optimizing flows based on successful resolutions and customer sentiment

AXO is currently onboarding select customers (April 2026) with general availability in H2 2026.

## Spiral by UJET

Spiral by UJET is a standalone conversational analytics and customer intelligence product, acquired by UJET in November 2025. Spiral processes 100% of customer conversations across all channels - voice, chat, email, surveys, and social media - with no sampling, no manual tagging, and no legacy taxonomy debt.

Spiral's architecture is built around three core pillars:

**1. Autonomous Taxonomy Generation**
Spiral uses advanced LLMs and clustering algorithms to build and continuously evolve the entire issue taxonomy automatically. No labels, no spreadsheets, zero bias. The taxonomy adapts as your business and customer issues change, immediately closing the 95% blind spot created by manual categorization.

**2. Ultra-Specific Issue Detection**
By processing 100% of conversations, Spiral enables early sparse signal detection - finding the critical issues mentioned by only a handful of customers that often become the next major operational fire. Spiral identifies the primary contact driver (Chief Complaint), sub-issues and user stories, and sparse emerging signals weeks before dashboards move.

**3. Searchable AI Agent (Explainable Intelligence)**
Instead of rigid BI dashboards, Spiral offers a plain-language AI interface that allows teams across Product, Operations, and Finance to ask complex questions and receive financially quantified answers:
- "Why did handle time spike last week?"
- "Which issues are driving refunds this month?"
- "What changed after our latest product release?"

Spiral delivers root-cause clarity no dashboard has ever provided - so every team knows what's happening, why it's happening, who it affects, and how to eliminate the problem at the source.

Spiral by UJET operates as both a standalone product and a deeply integrated component of UJET's broader AI platform. Customers include Turo, Owlet, and Whitepages.

## CRM-Native Architecture

UJET's CRM-native architecture is one of its most differentiated technical design decisions. Unlike traditional contact center platforms that store customer data in their own silos, UJET treats the CRM as the system of record - not the contact center. This means:

- No PII is stored in the contact center layer
- Customer context lives where it belongs - in the CRM or CDP
- Every interaction is tied to a unified customer record
- Agents work from a single, contextual view without switching systems
- Data sovereignty and compliance are structurally enforced, not bolted on

This architecture underpins UJET's security posture (SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI) and makes it uniquely suited for regulated industries including financial services, healthcare, and on-demand platforms.
